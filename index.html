Requirements for joining the Twitch Squad

These are small criteria that candidates who wish to join our team must meet. We can also accept streamers who are not members of the official DBD creators program if we find your channel interesting. But the program participants are still a priority.

You have at least 1,500 followers and a minimum of 30 online viewers on your channel.
Requirement
Streams on Dead by Daylight on your channel take from 80% of the time
Requirement
You don't have any other squads, or you have them, but after joining us, you will put our DBD CREATOR squad in the main one
